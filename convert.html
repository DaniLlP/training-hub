🩺 → 📄 Health Data Converter

Turns your big Apple Health export.zip into the tiny health_data.json your dashboard reads. Runs entirely on this device — nothing is uploaded.
1
In the Health app: tap your photo → Export All Health Data → save the export.zip to Files.
2
Tap the box below and pick that export.zip.
3
When it finishes, tap Copy JSON (or Save) and paste it into health_data.json on GitHub.
✅ Done —
To update GitHub from your phone: open your repo → tap health_data.json → ✏️ edit → select all → paste → Commit. Then open your dashboard and tap Load latest from GitHub (or just reload).
💡 Big exports (3M+ records) are processed in small pieces so this won't crash — it may take 20–90s on a phone. Keep this screen open while the bar fills.
