🔥 HYROX Open Men · sub-60 Blueprint

🎯 The sub-60 math
HYROX = 8×1km runs + 8 functional stations. A sub-60 needs your 8 runs to average ~4:45–5:00/km under fatigue, plus efficient stations (~26–28 min total work). Your VO₂max (59) and bootcamp base already cover the engine — the gap is compromised running (running fast with a high heart rate right after stations) and specific station skill.

🏅 Barcelona Marathon · sub-2:45 Blueprint

🎯 The sub-2:45 math
2:45:00 = 3:54 min/km (6:17/mile) held for 42.2 km. Your VO₂max supports it, but a marathon is won on durability: high weekly km, long runs with marathon-pace blocks, and fuelling the engine. Biggest lever for you → progressively raise running volume from the current ~3–4 runs/week toward 5–6, peaking near 90–100 km/week.
